Cookie settings

If you accept optional analytics, we use Microsoft Clarity heatmaps and session replays to understand clicks, scrolling and navigation and improve this website. Advertising consent stays denied. You can use Town Ledger without accepting.

Tracking is off in this local preview. Accepting saves a preview preference only. A live release would ask again.

Clarity may use analytics cookies. Masking does not make every aspect of a visit anonymous. See Microsoft’s Privacy Statement ↗.
